Frequently Asked Questions (Colombia Tours)

Key operational and logistical details for international visitors.

Is hotel pick-up and drop-off guaranteed for all tours?

Yes. All Transfers & Tours packages include door-to-door hotel pick-up and return transfers from hotels, Airbnb rentals, and accommodations in major urban districts of Bogotá, Medellín, and Cartagena [Source: Operations Department, Transfers & Tours Colombia].

What is the RNT License N° 44180 and why is it essential?

The Registro Nacional de Turismo (RNT N° 44180) is the legal operating license issued by Colombia's Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Tourism. Booking with an RNT-certified operator ensures compliant public tourism vehicles, licensed guides, and mandatory health & accident travel insurance.

How do I request vegetarian or special dietary meals for tours?

You can specify vegetarian, vegan, or food allergy preferences directly in the booking form or when confirming your reservation with your assigned support agent via WhatsApp.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We offer 100% free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to departure for all shared and private tours with no penalty fees.
